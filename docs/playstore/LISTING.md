## Full Description (4000 chars max)

🍽️ **Plately — The Intelligent Cooking Ecosystem**
Zero Waste, Maximum Taste.

Plately transforms your kitchen experience. Scan what you have, discover what to cook, and follow step-by-step guided cooking sessions — all powered by AI.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🧠 **SMART RECIPE MATCHING**
• Instantly see which recipes match your available ingredients
• AI ranks recipes by ingredient match percentage
• "Use It Up" tab prioritizes expiring ingredients to reduce food waste
• Browse by cuisine, difficulty, and cook time

📸 **SCAN & DETECT INGREDIENTS**
• Camera AI: Point your phone at ingredients for instant detection
• Receipt OCR: Scan grocery receipts to auto-add items to your shelf
• Barcode Scanner: Scan product barcodes for quick identification
• Manual Entry: Add ingredients by name with smart search

🗄️ **LIVING SHELF (Digital Pantry)**
• Track your fridge, freezer, and pantry inventory
• Automatic expiry alerts with freshness color coding
• Real-time sync across devices
• AI-predicted expiry dates

👨‍🍳 **GUIDED COOKING SESSIONS**
• Step-by-step cooking instructions with built-in timers
• Beginner Mode: Simplified instructions for new cooks
• AI Chat Assistant: Ask questions while cooking ("Can I substitute butter?")
• Voice Commands: Hands-free control while cooking
• Background cooking: Continue tracking even when you leave the app
• Persistent notification with Next/Pause/Stop controls

📊 **NUTRITION & HEALTH**
• Calorie tracker with daily macro breakdown
• Scan food photos for instant calorie estimation
• Weekly meal planner
• Auto-generated shopping lists

🌍 **MULTILINGUAL**
• English, Uzbek (Latin & Cyrillic), Russian, Korean
• AI-translated recipes in your preferred language

🏆 **GAMIFICATION**
• Earn XP for cooking, scanning, and exploring
• Level up and unlock badges
• Track your cooking streaks

🔐 **PRIVACY FIRST**
• Sign in with Google, email, or browse as guest
• Your data is encrypted and never sold
• Camera images processed in real-time, never stored
