# Privacy Policy for Plately

**Effective Date:** June 9, 2026
**Last Updated:** June 9, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Plately ("we", "our", "us") is a smart cooking app that helps you discover recipes based on ingredients you have, track your pantry inventory, and guide you through cooking. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- **Email address** (when signing in with email or Google)
- **Display name** and profile photo (from Google Sign-In)
- Anonymous guest accounts do not collect personal information

### 2.2 Usage Data
- Recipes viewed, cooked, and saved
- Cooking session history (steps completed, time spent)
- Flavor preferences and dietary settings
- App interactions for improving recommendations

### 2.3 Device Permissions
- **Camera**: Used to scan receipts, barcodes, and detect ingredients via AI vision. Photos are processed in real-time and are not stored on our servers.
- **Notifications**: Used to send cooking timer alerts and step-by-step guidance during active cooking sessions.
- **Location** (optional): Used to show nearby restaurants in Order mode. Never shared with third parties.

### 2.4 Inventory Data
- Ingredient names, quantities, and expiry dates you manually add or scan
- This data is stored securely to power recipe matching and expiry alerts

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Recipe Recommendations**: Match recipes to your available ingredients
- **Cooking Guidance**: Provide step-by-step instructions, timers, and AI cooking tips
- **Expiry Tracking**: Alert you about ingredients nearing expiration
- **Personalization**: Improve recipe suggestions based on your flavor profile and cooking history
- **Analytics**: Understand app usage patterns to improve the experience (aggregated, non-personal)

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- All data is stored securely using **Supabase** (hosted on AWS with encryption at rest and in transit)
- Authentication is handled by **Supabase Auth** with industry-standard encryption
- We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes

## 6. Data Retention

- Account data is retained as long as your account is active
- You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us
- Cooking session data is stored locally on your device and can be cleared at any time

## 7. Children's Privacy

Plately is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

- **Export** your data
